About Me

I'm Gagan — a Senior Software Engineer with 7 years of experience building event-driven microservices and cloud-native platforms in Go, Python, and TypeScript.

Currently at G2, I built Forge — an AI agent platform that automates the Jira-to-deployment lifecycle. Before that, I re-architected Gartner's CRM from legacy PHP to a Go-based system spanning 21 microservices and 49+ Lambda functions.

When I'm not writing code, you'll find me on a football pitch, watching a match (Visca el Barca!), or planning my next trip. I also tinker with side projects and think about how to make engineering teams ship faster.
